The Passport Page of a Country That Isn't There. A deterministic Node 22 program (stdlib only, no network) that draws one invented passport spread — the Republic of Kestria — and writes it to `/output/entry.png`: a bilingual data page with an engraved portrait, a machine-readable zone with computed check digits, and a visas page with a multiple-entry visa, five border stamps in a coherent travel history, and a fee stamp. An AI judge compares the images two at a time, and the 20 best win rates share $50.

Every date on the spread agrees: the passport was issued 12 MAR 2024, the Aldor visa was issued 03 MAY 2024, and every stamp falls between issue and the 11 MAR 2034 expiry, in a plausible travel order (Helvetia 28 MAY 2024, Aldor departure 14 JUN 2024, Nordmark 02 SEP 2024, Thalassia entry 11 NOV 2024, Thalassia exit 19 NOV 2024).

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

This writes `/output/entry.png` (1024x1024 PNG, under the 1 MiB cap). Running it twice produces byte-identical output.
